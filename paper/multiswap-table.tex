  \caption{Proving the swap-independent core of one MultiSwap
  \cite{multiswap} verification: two batched RSA-accumulator updates, each
  checked by a Wesolowski proof of exponentiation (four exponentiations with
  $352$-bit exponents modulo a $2048$-bit RSA modulus), plus a Poseidon-based
  hash-to-prime evaluation.  The four upper rows prove the same integer
  Mod-R1CS statement of $6209$ rows, taken from Limber's repository
  \cite{limber}, and were measured on the same machine (Apple M4, 16GB) with
  one and with eight threads, as medians of five runs; prover time includes
  the commitment and excludes witness generation (below $0.15$\,s in every
  row).  All four target $114$ bits of security; the \ftwoz\ and Zinc+ rows
  count $10$ and $14$ bits of proof-of-work grinding respectively.  The
  \ftwoz\ opening uses Ligerito in the unique-decoding regime at rate $1/8$
  with fold arity $4$ and fold grinding, validated at the $114$-bit target.

  constraints) quoted verbatim from \cite[Table 1]{limber}.
