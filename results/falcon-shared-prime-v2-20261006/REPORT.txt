Recorded 2026-10-06 UTC

Outcome

SharedPrimeV2 is implemented and pushed on codex/falcon-shared-prime.
The native backend remains the default. V2 passes the implemented correctness
checks, but does not pass the requested performance promotion gate. The full
remaining benchmark matrix and twenty-seed confirmation were skipped under
the requested regression policy; this report does not claim performance parity.

Implemented protocol

* Direct ring decoding retains the ideal quotient, cubic outer sumcheck and
  four endpoint answers. It uses beta^j, masked signature equality weights,
  storage weights (1,chi,chi^3,chi^2), signed source encodings and the S1 offset.
  Aliases accumulate in E before lifting coordinates. The geometric sum is
  division-free at beta=0,1,-1. The ten ring inner rounds and endpoint are gone.
* Grouped polynomial lifting and lazy verifier projection are retained. P is
  fixed before the single shared prime and alpha are derived. The optimized
  integer proofs and shared prime-field binder continue to use that prime.
* The 115-bit family is 2^114 <= p <= 2^115 - 2^102 - 1. The 8192-row bridge
  uses one u128 sum per column, one product family and original source slots.
  The verifier checks actual sum bounds, prime-field reconstruction and the
  original-bit terminal query. Native retains its existing two-limb path.
* Shared protocol/statement domains are V2. Proof containers and public entry
  points are retained; V2 requires one sum vector and native requires two.
  Old shared-prime proofs must be regenerated. The final shared PCS remains.

At batch 1024 the structural changes remove 682 stored ring bytes, reduce the
integer-sum component from 524,288 to 262,144 bytes, and reduce GKR messages
from 286 to 273. Other transcript-dependent components mean the complete
payload difference need not equal those structural savings.

Bounds and security

For every supported batch 1 through 1024, the exact-integer ledger tests check
both targets, the prime interval, integer bounds and the changed GKR geometry.
At maximum size H_P=222,928,181,554,839,552 and H_src=43,013,625,445;
every allowed prime exceeds max(2H_P,H_src). The prime cap satisfies the
conservative (8192+1)(p-1) < 2^128-1 guard. Each actual column-sum bound is
checked independently at verification.

The ring inner sumcheck error term is removed; cubic endpoint batching and
the degree-20 projection term remain. At 100 bits, arithmetic grinding is
zero and projection difficulty is 2. At 128 bits, retained prime-category
budgets add 11 grinding bits and projection difficulty is 25. The complete
conservative ledger minima are approximately 101.002291 and 129.070893 bits.
These are the repository's existing computational work-normalized grinding
bounds, not unconditional statistical security or an independent audit.
The probable-prime sampler retains its composite-error budget (at most
2^-144) and rejects sampling failure; no new sampler availability guarantee
is claimed.

Measurement procedure

Mac: M1 Max, 8 performance + 2 efficiency cores, 64 GB; measured at 8 threads.
Power report showed AC and 80% charge, without a thermal or performance
warning. Will: Ryzen 9 9950X3D; measured at 16 threads.
All compared binaries use Rust 1.98.1, release, falcon-hybrid, and
RUSTFLAGS='-C target-cpu=native', with matching Cargo.lock hashes. Separate
build directories and immutable retained executables were used; SHA-256,
revision and build provenance appear in mac-manifest.json and will/manifest.json.
Will ran in /home/john-wu/code/BitZ-pcs-run. The user's other checkout was not
modified. Mac qualification preceded will qualification.

Primary time is witness + commitment + proving, without subtracting grinding.
Proof verification is separate. Tracing is disabled. RSS is the external
process peak from the warmed process; setup and warmup remain part of that
peak. Payload is the canonical stored proof payload, excluding Falcon
transport framing and the public statement. Nonce accounting runs after the
timed region and does not alter proof transcripts.

100-bit discovery: batch 1024, five independent seeds 42..46, alternating
forward/reverse process order, one warmup and three measured proofs per process.
There were three native/V1/V2 orders and two V2/V1/native orders; V1 always
occupied the middle position, so this was not fully balanced by position. Each host completed
15 processes and verified all 60 proofs. Each statistical observation is the
median within a seed/process, not one repetition. The reported ratio is the
geometric mean of paired seed ratios; 95% intervals use 10,000 paired bootstrap
draws. Intervals are per metric/cell, not simultaneous or family-wise.
No held-out twenty-seed confirmation ran after the RSS gate failed.

Qualification scope and decision

Completed: direct-decoder milestone diagnostic; final correctness tests;
five-seed 100-bit batch-1024 discovery on Mac 8 threads and will 16 threads;
verified 128-bit early-stop diagnostics on those same configurations.
Not run after regression: batches 1,3,32 in the qualification matrix; the
one-thread qualification matrix; remaining 128-bit discovery seeds/measured
V2 processes; all twenty-seed confirmation runs. Smaller-batch correctness
proofs and compatibility checks are not substitutes for that qualification.

Do not promote V2. Native remains the default. The 100-bit will timing gain
and payload reductions are useful, but neither removes the RSS failure or the
large 128-bit grinding regression. Compaction redesign and PCS grinding
optimization remain outside this change.
