Falcon locality screen12 assessment — 2026-10-13

Conclusion: qualify norm4 as the strongest latency candidate, retain norm2 as a
lower-memory alternative, and keep collapse batching separate until its small
additional benefit repeats. Do not promote forest grain or dense compaction from
this screen. No production-default change is justified by this screen alone.

Scope and evidence
------------------
Untraced screen12 only: canonical Falcon-512/1024, batch1024, security128,
12 physical workers, repeated Prepared, seeds42/43/44. Each variant/degree has
3 processes, 2 warmups/process and 7 measured proofs/process (21 samples total).
Ordered worker CPUs: 6,7,4,5,2,3,0,1,14,15,12,13; main CPU6. All comparisons
below use the same phase, degree, mode and CPU placement. No traced timing or
prior-campaign timing is pooled into this assessment.

Runner validation reports756 verified proofs including warmups, complete proof
and payload equality against the six baseline input cases, matching input/source
identity, frozen binary hashes and raw command/output/parser provenance.
Pooled proof payload medians remain256368 bytes (512) and321304 bytes (1024);
per-seed byte lengths vary with the input and match the corresponding baseline.

Control qualification matters: candidate-control total is0.99%/1.41% higher
than the frozen baseline for512/1024. Its per-seed differences are mixed, so
this screen does not establish a cause. A win against control alone is not
equivalent to a net production improvement. Keep both comparisons in follow-up.

Primary observations
--------------------
1. norm4: totals31.176/55.130ms; lower than control by2.20%/3.99% and frozen
   baseline by1.23%/2.64%. All six per-seed total medians improve against both
   references. Versus control, prove changes-2.18%/-5.38%, commit-0.54%/+9.34%,
   and verify-2.54%/-0.85%. The1024 total already includes the commit increase.
   Do not add separately computed stage medians to reconstruct total medians.
   Process-peak medians increase89,020→108,452KiB (+21.83%) and
   177,468→209,576KiB (+18.09%); these are per-process maxima including warmups.
   Retained RSS after verification falls45,352→43,192KiB and81,040→65,296KiB.
   Peak and retained memory describe different points in the lifecycle.

2. norm2: totals31.246/55.780ms, -1.98%/-2.86% versus control and
   -1.01%/-1.50% versus frozen baseline. All six seed medians improve against
   control; five of six improve against baseline (512 seed42 is+0.70%).
   Process-peak medians fall to84,808/167,036KiB (-4.73%/-5.88% versus control).
   norm4 is only0.070ms faster than norm2 at512 in the pooled total, and0.650ms
   faster at1024. This is a real memory/latency choice to remeasure, not a reason
   to discard norm2. norm3 is slower than both at1024 and has higher peak RSS.

3. collapse4: totals31.535/56.700ms, -1.08%/-1.26% against control, but only
   -0.09%/+0.13% against baseline. Five of six seed medians improve against
   control, only three of six against baseline. collapse8 is slightly lower
   at512 but higher at1024; neither shows a clear cross-degree net advantage.
   Trace collapse itself, then qualify norm4 alone alongside norm4+collapse4
   to determine whether batching contributes beyond the norm improvement.

4. Dense compaction: compact4 is-0.72%/-0.12% against control, but
   +0.26%/+1.28% against baseline. Its1024 seed medians are mixed against
   control and all higher than baseline. compact2/8 have no consistent
   cross-degree advantage. Keep off; profiles can distinguish copy cost from
   later-fold locality changes without treating trace wall time as qualification.

5. Forest grain: forest2 is+0.55%/-1.29% against control and+1.54%/+0.10%
   against baseline; all512 seed medians exceed baseline. Larger grains do
   not improve both degrees. Keep grain1. A1024 control-only difference does
   not justify enabling it for512 or another worker count.

Limits and next comparison
--------------------------
This is one repeated-mode screen at12 workers, with three independent processes
per variant/degree and correlated samples within each process. These descriptive
ratios make no statistical-significance claim. They do not establish cold-start
behavior,1/8/16-worker results, partial batches or other security settings.
Profiles can localize the changes but have instrumentation overhead. Qualify
norm4 and the proposed combination against same-phase baseline/control across
workers, keeping isolated norm4 as an anchor and norm2 as the memory alternative.
No benchmark, build, prover, test or implementation change was performed for
 this analysis; only the existing runner summaries and metadata were read.
