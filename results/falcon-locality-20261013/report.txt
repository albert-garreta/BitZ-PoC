Falcon locality and arithmetic optimization results — 2026-10-13

Enable four native square-norm prefix rounds for Falcon-512/8 workers and
Falcon-1024/12 workers. Both improve total prover latency in both confirmation
passes and all 24 selected per-seed comparisons. These are prover-latency
tradeoffs: verification and memory costs are reported below.

Falcon-512/12 retains its original prefix 1: the prefix 2/4 candidates offered
small prover gains but lost them in verification after proving. Other parallel
cases and the existing serial policy retain their previous prefix selection.
The new parallel policy requires K=1, capacity 1024 and modulus>=2^125. It
applies to padded batches 513..1024; performance qualification is at batch 1024.

Forest row grain, binding compaction and batched ring projection are implemented
and tested as bench-internals experiments. They did not establish reliable
production gains. Defaults remain grain 1, compaction off, collapse batch 1.
Finer binding/norm profiling and the reproducible experiment runner are retained.

COSTS / INTERPRETATION
- 512/8 cold verification after proving rises 11.85–12.02% (accepted2:
  8.209→9.181 ms). Combined commit+prove+verify rises 0.41–0.85%. Repeated
  combined latency improves 0.99–1.22%, with essentially unchanged peak RSS.
- 1024/12 repeated peak RSS rises 18.28–18.57%, approximately 32 MiB
  (accepted2:173.12→204.77 MiB). Cold peak falls about 2.8%. Cold verification
  rises 3.23–3.77%, but combined latency improves 1.54–2.17% repeated and
  1.60–2.44% cold. One repeated seed has a small combined-latency increase.
- Verification follows proving in the same process. An isolated verifier
  benchmark was not performed. No allocator/cache cause is established.
- Cold means a fresh process/Prepared; CPU caches were not flushed. Repeated
  samples share Prepared. Peak RSS includes warmups and all process work;
  retained RSS is a distinct measure. Setup is excluded from prover latency.
- Each latency cell uses 3 seeds with 7 measured samples per seed (21 total);
  repeated cases have 2 warmups per seed. No outliers were discarded. These
  are descriptive ratios, not significance tests or cross-hardware claims.

STAGE EVIDENCE / REJECTED EXPERIMENTS
Production-light profiles put 1024/12 norm time at 3.350→2.247 ms. Unchanged
512/12 norm is 2.146→2.125 ms. Forest remains largest at about 14.5/27.0 ms.
Traced profiles are separate diagnostics; nested stages overlap and cannot
be added to total latency. Other stage changes do not establish a mechanism.
Matched candidate-v1 profiles found:
- Compaction added 0.284/0.566 ms copying for 512/1024 against only 0.030/0.089 ms
  saved in later folds. It retains vector capacity.
- Collapse batch 4 changed collapse 1.366/2.720→1.379/2.770 ms.
- Larger forest tasks produced mixed or regressing total/stage results.
  Grain 16 is implemented/tested but not separately measured; its clamped
  policy equals grain 8 for the measured 12-worker geometries.
