Falcon production1/production2 assessment — 2026-10-13

Conclusion: the final production binary does not reproduce a uniform benefit
for every parallel configuration qualified with the experimental binary.
The clearest repeatable gains are Falcon512/8 workers and both degrees/12.
A conservative next selector would enable the new parallel norm4 path only
for those three measured cells, then reconfirm the rebuilt production binary.
Falcon512/16 is weaker but improves both pooled totals; Falcon1024/8 and/16
are inconclusive across the two passes and incur consistently higher peak RSS.
No measurement is discarded and no phases are pooled in the tables below.

Configuration findings
----------------------
Falcon512/8: total commit+prove improves1.52% and1.46% in the two passes,
with all6 seed medians lower. Process-peak medians are essentially unchanged.
This is the clearest small improvement without a peak-memory cost.

Falcon512/12: improves4.37% and2.98%, with all6 seed medians lower.
Falcon1024/12: improves2.70% and3.95%, also with all6 seed medians lower.
Peak RSS increases22.86–23.34% for512 and18.22–18.42% for1024. These
improvements consistently cover the observed1024 commit-time increase.

Falcon512/16: pooled totals improve2.69% and0.53%, but only4/6 seed medians
are lower. production1 seed43 increases1.00%; production2 seed42 increases
0.50%. Peak RSS rises22.01–22.39%. This is weaker evidence than the three
cells above. Keeping it requires accepting a small, variable benefit for a
material peak-memory increase; a strict conservative promotion would omit it.

Falcon1024/8: production1 regresses2.55% pooled, with seed42/43 regressions
2.55%/2.02% and seed44 improvement0.64%. production2 improves1.20% pooled
and all3 seeds improve1.00–1.45%. Thus the first regression does not repeat,
but neither does a stable net improvement across the two complete phases.
Peak RSS rises18.45–18.68% in both. Keeping the previous prefix at this shape
is justified without assuming a cause for the discrepant latency results.

For the production1 Falcon1024/8 regression, pooled prove rises
47.580644→48.372380ms (+1.66%), commit3.558403→3.900088ms (+9.60%) and
verify16.450499→16.610422ms (+0.97%). In production2 prove decreases
2.08%, commit still increases8.59% and verify decreases3.88%. The higher
commit cost persists but the prove component changes direction. Minor-fault
medians rise35,840→41,028 in production1; retained RSS falls79,060→63,200KiB
while process peak rises175,796→208,236KiB. These observations do not prove
a causal explanation such as allocator placement or page-fault cost.

Falcon1024/16: production1 improves0.65% pooled and all3 seeds; production2
regresses0.13% pooled, with seed42/43 increases0.56%/2.33% and seed44
improvement1.01%. Peak rises17.33–17.71% and commit rises8.47–10.94%.
This is inconclusive for a default promotion, similar to the8-worker case.
Production2 seed43 is retained:49.089094→50.235209ms total, prove
45.274006→46.023993ms and commit3.796754→4.127849ms. No diagnosis is
established from these summaries, and no unfavorable sample was removed.

Serial: the existing width4 policy is unchanged. Pooled differences are small
and individual seeds move in both directions, including production2 Falcon1024
seed43 (+1.56%). These remain in the record and are not evidence of a new
serial improvement or a proven absence of variation.

Verification, memory and proof sizes
------------------------------------
No verifier algorithm was changed. Verify medians are mixed: Falcon512/12
is+0.11% then+3.60%, while Falcon1024/12 is-2.85% then-4.01%. Falcon1024/8
is+0.97% then-3.88%; Falcon1024/16 is+1.77% then-0.24%. Do not claim
universal verifier-speed improvement or regression-free verifier timings.
The full table reports verifier and commit deltas alongside prove and total.

Process-peak RSS is a lifetime high-water measure including warmups. Retained
RSS after proof and verification often falls even when peak rises. Do not
conflate either with intentionally cached scratch. Unavailable production
scratch-capacity metrics are null, not zero. Payload medians remain256368
bytes for512 and321304 for1024; matching seeds preserve full proof identity.

Limits and next step
--------------------
These are descriptive medians from two independent run phases, with samples
correlated within each process. No statistical-significance claim is made.
Do not average the two phase ratios, pool away regressions, or remove the
elevated process measurements. If narrowing the selector, use a new frozen
production artifact and retain these results as evidence for the earlier one.
The proposed row_vars==10 guard distinguishes Falcon512 norm rows from
Falcon1024 row_vars==11; adding row_vars to the selector must preserve the
previous serial/rejection behavior and other parameter guards.
No builds, tests, provers or benchmark processes were launched for this review.
