Falcon locality experimental findings — 2026-10-13

Decision: keep forest grain 1, dense compaction off, and collapse batch 1.
The measured alternatives do not establish a reliable production improvement.
Norm-prefix selection and final production confirmation are separate work; this
note evaluates the frozen candidate-v1 experiments only. No benchmarks, builds,
Rust tests, or source/runner edits were performed to produce this assessment.

Method and provenance
---------------------
Canonical v9, batch 1024 / security 128, 12 physical workers with ordered CPUs
[6,7,4,5,2,3,0,1,14,15,12,13], main CPU 6. Comparisons match phase, degree,
mode, CPU placement and seed; seeds are 42/43/44. Screen and qualification use
2 warmups + 7 timed proofs per seed/process, giving 21 timed proofs per cell.
Profiles use 1 warmup + 3 timed proofs per seed, giving 9 timed proofs per cell.
Warmups are excluded. Repeated samples share Prepared and are correlated.
Negative changes mean lower elapsed time; ratios are descriptive, not claims
of statistical significance. No cold-start conclusion follows from these phases.

Each experiment changes one explicit control selector; combined changes norm
prefix 4 and collapse4. Candidate control is NOT the frozen baseline binary.
Screen control itself is 0.99%/1.41% above baseline at N512/N1024, with mixed
per-seed differences. Control-only wins must not be reported as production wins.

Completed source phases contain 2196 verified proofs including warmups:
screen12: 756; profile12: 144; qualify: 1296 across 1/8/12/16 workers. These counts
include norm variants and other worker cells; tables below select 12 workers.

This note reads that existing validation; it does not independently rerun it.
Debug digest equality is not a direct comparison of serialized proof bytes.

Forest interpretation: grain2 lowers N1024 versus control for all three seeds,
but its pooled result is still 0.10% above baseline. N512 is slower pooled and
all three N512 grain2 seed medians exceed baseline. Grain4/8 do not supply a
cross-degree benefit. The traced forest2 stage also has mixed seed directions.
Keep the default grain 1. This does not prove all grain choices lose everywhere.

Grain 16 was implemented/test-covered but NOT benchmarked: it appears in
configs.json and no completed phase manifest. At 12 workers, the row/task cap
makes requested grains 1/2/4/8/16 become 1/2/4/4/4 for 128 rows (N512), and
1/2/4/8/8 for 256 rows (N1024), on eligible >=4 MiB passes. Smaller later passes
can clamp further. Therefore 16 selects the same effective policy as 8 here;
no distinct measured 16 result is claimed. Requested 4 and 8 also coincide on
the N512 128-row shape, so timing differences do not establish a grain effect.

Compaction interpretation: compact4 improves N512 versus control in all three
seeds, but its pooled result remains 0.26% above baseline. All N1024 compact4
seed medians exceed baseline, and control comparisons are mixed. The profile
explains a concrete cost: the added serial copy exceeds the observed reduction
in later dense folds. It truncates live length without releasing allocation
capacity. Keep off; parallel/out-of-place alternatives were not evaluated.

Collapse interpretation: collapse4/8 produce small pooled decreases against
control, but near-zero or positive baseline changes. N1024 seed directions are
mixed. The stage profile shows no faster collapse, and follow-up qualification
does not establish a consistent additional benefit on top of norm4. Keep batch 1.

PCS diagnostics and next bounded hypothesis
------------------------------------------
baseline-profiles/assessment.txt reports whole PCS 3.077/4.041 ms and aggregated
induced-basis 0.46/0.61 ms for N512/N1024 (nine traced samples each at 12 workers).
The source derivation in pcs-dispatch.txt proves initial sparse NTT is already
selected: message logs 13/14, rate exponent 1, 309 queries against 112/120 dispatch
cutoffs. Recursive levels use dense construction. The aggregate timer does not
identify a dominant level, allocation or reduction, and the 0.59/0.56 ms residual
cannot be assigned to NTT. Query/fold grinding must not be mistaken for pure
field arithmetic; textual timers are rounded to 0.01 ms.

A separate hypothesis is one full-domain sparse-transpose buffer with active
256-element windows, replacing HashMap/per-window allocations and densification
copy. Full buffers are 256/512 KiB, with 64/128 possible windows. Actual active
window counts were not measured. Keep weights, within-window arithmetic, output
basis, enforced sum and transcript identical. PCS remains unchanged here;
neither this idea nor a PCS speedup was implemented or demonstrated.
