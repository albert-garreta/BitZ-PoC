Forest bounds and ownership
- src/bitz/row_dispatch.rs:10 bounds grain using public row count, matrix bytes,
  requested grain, and worker count. Requested values are restricted to
  1/2/4/8/16 at lines 22 and 65. Serial and below-4-MiB passes retain grain 1.
  The divisor is nonzero after the workers<=1 guard; saturating multiplication
  cannot wrap it to zero. max(1) makes ilog2 defined, including zero rows. The
  resulting shift fits usize and the grain stays positive and power-of-two.
- The cap allows at least two potential tasks per worker when rows permit;
  this is a splitting bound, not a guarantee of two actually scheduled tasks.
  No worker identity or divisibility assumption is introduced. CyclicRows at
  row_dispatch.rs:87 retains its existing power-of-two-worker and divisible-row
  requirements, and remains the earlier dispatch branch in both kernels.
- forest.rs:1307 and kernels.rs:283 change only the indexed iterator's minimum
  split length. Existing disjoint mutable row chunks, row indexes, scalar/SIMD
  arithmetic, output dimensions, partial collection, and reduction stay intact.
  Existing matrix-byte expressions are reused from the preceding dispatch
  checks; there is no newly expanded arithmetic or allocation-size domain.
  Dense single-row chunking remains unchanged because its effective grain is 1.
- The row_dispatch module and new call sites are guarded by feature=parallel

  no Rayon dependency added by this change. Non-bench builds return grain 1;

- validate_result (:200) rejects failed processes, mismatched manifest/job,
  command/raw/process hashes, or saved trials that differ from renormalized raw
  output. validate_all (:218) rejects changed parsers/helpers/binaries, incomplete
  or extra results, inconsistent inputs/source roots or complete proof digest/

- Summary groups separate phases, variants, workers, cold/repeated and trace.
  Samples exclude warmups; per-seed medians are retained. Cold means first proof
  in a fresh process/Prepared object, not flushed hardware/page caches. Repeated
  samples share Prepared with warmups. Process peak RSS is a lifetime high-water
  mark, not a per-proof allocation measurement.
- profile_samples (:280) requires the expected number of prover roots and takes
  only each sampled root/descendants. It excludes warmups/verifier spans and
  preserves but ignores plaintext diagnostics. Nanosecond fields are converted
  to ms. Nested elapsed spans overlap, and summed worker time is not wall time;
  both caveats are recorded in the profile summary. LIG_PROVE_TRACE adds work,
  so those runs are for attribution, not latency qualification.
- The synthetic tests cover polluted parent environments, frozen parser/binary
  changes, wrong topology/security/protocol/order, missing baseline/stale results,
  proof mismatch, raw-command changes, and trace filtering. The new test at
  test_falcon_locality_optimization.py:107 checks all 15 locality variants and
  diagnostic isolation. These tests were inspected, not independently executed.
- Some selectors intentionally coincide on a given shape: for example norm4

  Treat these as control-equivalence observations, not independent speedups.
