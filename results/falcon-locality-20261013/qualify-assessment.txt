Norm4 selection
---------------

                  8 workers      12 workers      16 workers
  Falcon512        1.10%            4.09%            1.70%
  Falcon1024       0.84%            2.48%            1.66%
Every one of the18 underlying seed medians also decreases against baseline.
The smallest seed decrease is0.27% (512,16 workers,seed42); the largest is
4.35% (512,12 workers,seed42). All18 also decrease against candidate control,
although one control process is anomalous as documented below. These are
descriptive measured differences, not statistical-significance claims.

The proposed/source selector chooses width4 for K==1, instance_vars==10,
field modulus>=2^125 and workers8/12/16. It retains the previous one-worker
width4 square-norm /width3 rejection policy, and leaves other cases unchanged.
The parallel selector matches the measured full batch1024/security128 shape.
instance_vars is a padded-capacity condition: throughput for partial batches
with the same capacity, other field moduli above the threshold, or unmeasured
worker counts is not established by this campaign. Correctness tests cover
representation equivalence independently; this report does not claim to rerun
them. Final production-default measurements remain the confirmation step.

At one worker both frozen baseline/control already select norm4. Explicit
norm4 changes pooled totals by+0.23% (512) /-0.18% (1024) versus baseline,
while all six serial per-seed comparisons are slightly higher. This is not a
new serial optimization. In particular, pooled medians are not averages of
per-seed medians. Retain every measurement rather than selecting a favorable
aggregation. Explicit norm2 is about1% slower serially.

Tradeoffs to report
-------------------
Peak RSS: norm4 process-peak medians are essentially unchanged for512 at
8 workers, but increase23.16%/22.04% at12/16 workers relative to control.
For1024 they increase18.79%/18.06%/18.08% at8/12/16 workers. These are
process-lifetime maxima including warmups, not retained buffer capacity.
Retained RSS is lower at12/16 workers for512 and all parallel counts for1024.
Do not infer lower process peak merely from a smaller materialized norm table.

Falcon1024 commit time increases with norm4:3.551→3.911ms (8 workers),
3.820→4.147ms (12),3.824→4.144ms (16) versus control, while prove time
decreases47.476→46.891ms,53.529→51.143ms,45.285→43.803ms respectively.
Total commit+prove still improves at every parallel cell and seed. The summaries
do not establish the reason for the commit change; do not attribute it to new
commit arithmetic. Each total is measured per proof, not reconstructed by
adding independent stage medians.

Verifier timings are mixed against baseline (for example512/12 is+1.48%,
1024/16 is+0.20%, while several other cells are lower). No verifier algorithm
was optimized by this selector, and this phase does not establish a universal
verifier-speed improvement. Keep final production verify checks in the report.

Other candidates
----------------
collapse4 alone does not consistently improve either degree across workers.
Adding it to norm4 changes total medians versus norm4 by:
                    8 workers      12 workers      16 workers
  Falcon512          -0.08%           +1.39%           -0.41%
  Falcon1024         +0.40%           -0.16%           +0.19%
Its per-seed gains are also mixed. Leave collapse batch1; the combination does
not provide a consistent extra benefit and is slower at512/12.

norm2 is a reasonable lower-memory alternative for further specialized work:
it lowers process-peak medians for512/12,512/16 and every parallel1024 cell.
It is slower than norm4 in all six parallel pooled totals. It also has a
1024/16 seed43 baseline regression (+3.38%), so avoid declaring it uniformly
better from pooled totals alone. No additional defaults are selected here.
Dense compaction and forest grain were excluded after their prior screen;
qualification supplies no new evidence to promote them.

Limits
------
Only repeated operation has been qualified here. No cold-run claim is made.
The source promotion and final production binary can have different code
layout from this benchmark-feature binary; the two planned production passes
are required confirmation. Keep those phases separate and retain outliers.
No builds, tests, provers or benchmark processes were launched for this analysis.
