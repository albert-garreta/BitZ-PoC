Algebra and accumulation bounds
- PreparedFixedWordWeights is constructed through its existing checked
  constructor, which limits TERMS to16. Its fields are private. The new
  unsigned_word_dots helper accepts1..8 lanes at compile time and keeps a
  separate UintAccumulator<L,1> for every lane.
- Each lane receives exactly the same TERMS unsigned-word products as the
  scalar unsigned_dot path, in ascending term order. Only independent lanes
  are interleaved. No lanes or carries are combined.
- Every prepared weight is a residue below p. For an unsigned u64 input,
  S < TERMS*p*2^64 <=16*p*2^64 = p*2^68. For L>=2, R=2^(64L)>=2^128,
  so S<pR and the existing single Montgomery REDC in finish_words is valid.
  The accumulator has L+2 words, exceeding the at-most64L+68-bit bound.
  L=1 continues through the existing general remainder/canonical conversion
  path. TERMS=0 returns zero through the same reduction path.
- Prepared weights retain their existing extra Montgomery factor; reusing
  finish_words produces the same field representation as scalar evaluation.
  The helper introduces no ISA-specific arithmetic, unsafe accesses, or
  narrowing conversion. Field-kernel portability remains that of the existing
  UintAccumulator/finish_words implementations.

Ring integration, lane order, and tails
- collapse_weights uses indexed par_chunks_exact and collects fixed arrays in
  input-chunk order. Lane i reads coordinate vector chunk[i] for every term.
  Vec::into_flattened preserves that order without manual layout conversion.
  The scalar remainder appends the final original vectors in order.
- Production vectors have batch*N entries, divisible by2/4/8 for both Falcon
  degrees. Empty and arbitrary short/tail slices are also handled correctly.
- The associated prepared type is fixed by Cfg and PreparedLinearCombination;
  the inherent public method is available on that concrete type without
  exporting a new ring-facing representation or constructing it manually.
- xi powers, prepared weights, signed carry/target computation, coefficient
  dimensions, and canonical native-coordinate interpretation are unchanged.

Transcript and default policy
- Both prover and verifier select a computational batch size and then call the
  same existing validation/challenge/absorption sequence. Carries are absorbed
  and projection grinding completes before xi is sampled. Batching only changes
  evaluation of the already-fixed coordinate projection after that challenge.
- The benchmark selector accepts1/2/4/8 and defaults to1; production without
  bench-internals always selects1. The1 path preserves the original scalar
  prepared-combination loop. No proof fields, security parameters, equations,
  transcript labels, or verification checks change.

Inspected tests and limits
- Field tests compare independent-lane output both to ordinary field arithmetic
  and scalar prepared dots. They cover0/1/11/16 terms,1/2/4/8 lanes, L=1/L=2,
  small and near-word-boundary moduli, zero/negative-one coefficients, and
  operands through u64::MAX; callback order/count is checked.
- Ring tests cover coordinate bounds, xi=0/1/p-1, both61-/127-bit prime paths,
  all supported lane widths, empty/short/tail slices through257 entries, and
  pools with1/4/12 workers. Complete ring proofs and transcript continuations
  are compared across widths, with partial batches, grinding, and changed
  carries checked against the returned relation.
- These are inspected tests, not reviewer-observed passes. Central native and
  portable builds/tests must establish execution. The review establishes no
  performance improvement; lane interleaving can increase register pressure,
  and the scalar-tail extension can reallocate for arbitrary non-Falcon tails.
