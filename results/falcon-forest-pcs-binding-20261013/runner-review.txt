Two findings were reported to the runner author and fixed before qualification:

1. Inherited dependency selectors could contaminate frozen experiment settings.
   The original child-environment filter stripped BITZ_/F2_/OBLONG_ but left
   FLOCK_COMMIT_TIMING, NTT_DEEP_NOFUSE, LIG_LOOKAHEAD_DISABLE, and other runtime
   flags. These affect actual commit timing/kernel/PCS behavior; examples are
   src/hybrid/opening.rs:1517,
   vendor/flock-mod/crates/flock-core/src/ntt/additive_ntt_f128.rs:446,
   vendor/flock-mod/crates/flock-core/src/pcs/ligerito.rs:3795.
   Resuming with a differently configured shell could therefore mix timings
   under the same manifest. The final subprocess_env helper (runner:39-55)
   removes all current dependency selector/trace prefixes before applying
   recorded overrides. A polluted-parent test exercises actual environment
   construction for both baseline and candidate (tests:115).

2. Summarize-only did not enforce the recorded runner/helper provenance.
   A synthetic changed-current-runner hash was accepted by validate_all before
   the fix, even though changed profile parsing could alter the summary under
   frozen provenance. The final validate_all checks parser_provenance against
   every manifest before reinterpreting evidence (runner:200-206). Tests now
   reject both a changed runner hash and a changed normalization-helper hash
   (tests:128). Old evidence requires its exact recorded parser revision.
