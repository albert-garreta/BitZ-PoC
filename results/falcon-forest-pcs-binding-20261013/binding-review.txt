Accumulator bounds
------------------
word_binding.rs:570 limits each task to 1024/N instances, hence at most 1024
coefficient pairs across both sides. Decoded values are in [-16384,16383], and
their difference is in [-32767,32767]. Each sign accumulator is bounded by:
  c0:       1024 * (p-1) * 16384
  c2:       1024 * (p-1) * 32767
  full sum: 2048 * (p-1) * 16384
All are below 2^153 for p < 2^128. FpLinearAcc<2,1> wraps
UintAccumulator<2,1>, with two low limbs, one high limb, and one carry limb
(vendor/field/src/integer/product.rs:81). No machine-integer wrap is possible
under this task bound. Positive/negative totals are reduced before the final
field sum. The duplicate-product option adds the separately reduced even sum
and preserves the same initial claim.

12-worker fallback and experiment qualification
----------------------------------------------
arithmetic.rs:770-771 continues to select Legacy at 12 workers by default.
Legacy construction returns initial=None (1066-1101), so enabling construction
fusion alone at 12 workers deliberately falls back to the existing first-round
pass. An experiment intended to exercise fusion at 12 must also force optimized
construction, e.g. BITZ_BENCH_BINDER_CONSTRUCTION=on (or the broader binding
strategy override). Comparing fused and unfused at the same forced construction
strategy avoids attributing the strategy change to fusion.
