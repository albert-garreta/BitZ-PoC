IMPLEMENTED
100-bit target: the capped 115-bit interval [2^114, 2^115-2^102-1], one
unsplit u128 sum vector, zero arithmetic grinding, projection difficulty 2.
128-bit target: [2^125, 2^126-1], two bounded limbs, the native arithmetic
schedule (removing V2's extra 11 bits), projection difficulty 14.
Both retain direct ring decoding, grouped polynomial lifting, optimized integer
proofs, the shared binder and PCS opening. The one shared prime is sampled after
P is fixed and used by both arithmetic branches. Prepared parameters select the
bridge; V3 transcript domains bind the interval, target, geometry and schedule.
