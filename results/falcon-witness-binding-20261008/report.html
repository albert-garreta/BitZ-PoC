Retained configuration

Arithmetic uses direct validation and prepared decoder/padding/slack values, with group cap 1. Full Falcon additionally uses bounded coefficient reductions, coefficient-oriented replay, grouped integer prefixes, and the ring K4 adapter; caps are 16 for Falcon-512 and 8 for Falcon-1024. Unsupported workload shapes and platforms retain the reference path.

Rejected fusion, intermediate experimental replay, and stage/mask/environment controls were deleted. The retained numerical loops, verifier behavior, packing, PCS algorithms, security schedule, and norm protocol were preserved. The cleanup received static Rust Reviewer and Simplify Rust review and formatting; it was not rebuilt or rebenchmarked, as requested.
