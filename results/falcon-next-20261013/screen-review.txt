Bundle warning: N1024/12 `all` is 49.660 ms versus own-tables 49.684 ms: only
0.05% faster, with mixed seeds (+0.27%, +0.33%, -0.94%). Median verification is
15.924 versus 15.351 ms (+3.74%); commit+prove+verify is 65.552 versus 65.314 ms
(+0.36%). Thus the large `all` gain versus control is not evidence to promote
norm, dense and PCS together. At eight workers `all` is +0.46% N512 and +3.13%
N1024; the latter is slower in every seed.

Limits for final reporting: cold proofs are not covered yet; samples within a
seed/process are correlated; per-seed agreement is not a significance test.
Comparison-file lifecycle is median(per-proof total_prover_ms + verify_ms), not
the sum of their medians and not input generation/preparation/resource sampling.
Peak figures are medians of process maxima including warmups, not allocation
counts or necessarily the active phase's live memory. Retained RSS includes
inputs/allocator/Prepared and differs from cached-vector capacity. For example,
confirmation N1024/12 own-tables retains 66.16 MiB versus ownership 64.52 MiB,
despite similar process peaks (205.35 versus 205.03 MiB). Minor faults are counts.
Use same-binary control for experimental causality, and retain matched frozen
baseline comparisons for final production claims; neither zero regressions nor
an across-worker improvement has been established here.
