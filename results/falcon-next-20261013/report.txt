Falcon forest, binding, norm and PCS optimization qualification
2026-10-13 — falcon-poc

Outcome
All five proposed experiments are implemented and independently tested.
Qualified defaults improve full-batch arithmetic Falcon proving at 12 workers
by approximately 8-9% in the confirmation run. Eight-worker medians are flat.
There is a small unresolved Falcon-1024/16 performance regression: 1.25% and
1.60% slower in two untraced runs. Verification at 12 workers is also slightly
slower. These results support the selected 12-worker paths, not a claim of zero
performance regressions. No correctness/proof-content regression was found.

Production defaults
1. Persistent 64-shard forest ownership at exactly 12 workers, at least 64 rows
   divisible by 64 and a matrix of at least 4 MiB. Ownership remains stable for
   compatible high-bit folds. Smaller shapes retain the previous dispatcher.
2. Factored L0-L2 forest lookup tables at 12 workers on the native x86 backend
   (pclmulqdq, sse4.1, avx512f, avx512bw, vpclmulqdq). Each byte lookup is the
   sum of two 16-entry nibble lookups. Portable/other worker pools retain the
   expanded table default. Selection follows the current pool on every proof.
3. Direct compact binding-fold output only for Falcon-512, live batch exactly
   1024, 12 workers, and field modulus >=2^125. The extra half-sized allocation
   is local to the proof. Production drops both binding buffers after the proof;
   only when experimental scratch reuse is enabled does the original larger
   allocation return to the sole Prepared cache. Other cases keep the strided fold.
4. K=1 native norm-prefix aggregation is implemented but remains experimental.
5. PCS sparse transpose directly into the final allocation is implemented but
   remains experimental.

The forest guards apply to eligible BitZ forests generally, not just Falcon.
Performance qualification is limited to the Falcon workload/host below; these
measurements do not establish gains for other forests or portable CPUs.
No protocol, security ledger, transcript order, grinding rule or proof format
was changed. The production default remains canonical arithmetic Falcon v9.

Why two experiments remain off
The screen/confirm/lifecycle phases used a separate frozen bench-internals
binary and explicit per-experiment controls. They are not production timings.
Norm batching did not add a reliable gain on top of forest improvements:
N1024/12 ownership+tables49.684 ->50.335ms with batching, all seeds slower.
At512/12 automatic width 1 bypasses native-prefix batching; forced width 4 is
31.362ms legacy versus31.445ms batched. Width selection is a different change.
PCS direct output also failed the complete-workload test: after forest changes,
N1024/12 repeated 49.488 ->50.097ms, all seeds slower;512 repeated gain was only
0.16% with mixed seeds. Direct compact binding output was not promoted for 1024
or8 workers because combined/confirmation gains did not hold there.

Benchmark overrides (only with bench-internals):
BITZ_FOREST_TABLES=expanded|factored
BITZ_FOREST_ROW_OWNERSHIP=legacy|shards64
BITZ_ARITHMETIC_NATIVE_NORM=legacy|batched
BITZ_ARITHMETIC_DENSE_OUTPUT=inplace|compact
BITZ_PCS_SPARSE_TRANSPOSE=legacy|direct
The old dense-compaction override suppresses the new implicit compact default;
explicit compact output plus an explicit old compaction threshold is rejected.

Correctness and review
All 16 campaign phases completed with 3414 verified proofs, including warmups:
1641 Falcon-512 and1773 Falcon-1024. Input/source roots, complete in-build Debug
proof digests, payload totals, binary hashes, raw/command/process hashes
and frozen parser provenance match; each raw payload breakdown sums to its
reported total. Digest equality checks whole proof values;
it is not a direct comparison of a canonical serialized wire format.
Full-batch 128-bit payload medians remain 256368 bytes for 512 and 321304 bytes
for 1024. Across the three seeds, payload sizes range 256112-256848 bytes for 512
and 321272-321432 bytes for 1024, with exact equality per matched input.
Experimental all-options checks cover degrees 512/1024, workers 8/12/16,
(batch,security)=(3,128),(513,128),(513,100),(1024,100). Final production checks
cover both degrees at 12 workers with(513,128),(513,100),(1024,100), in addition
to 128-bit full-batch performance campaigns at 1/8/12/16. These small correctness
campaigns use one fresh proof per seed/configuration, not21 latency samples.

Final production tests:371 Falcon passed (3 existing ignored);103 BitZ passed

Methodology and reproduction
AMD Ryzen 9 9950X3D; Linux 6.17.0-41; rustc 1.98.1, LLVM 22.1.8; native target.
Worker CPU order[6,7,4,5,2,3,0,1,14,15,12,13,10,11,8,9], truncated to workers;
main/auxiliary CPU6. Eight workers fit the96MiB-cache CCD;12 spans two CCDs.
Cold means fresh process/Prepared, not flushed OS/CPU caches. Repeated cells
use2 warmups+7 measured samples per seed42/43/44 (21 samples). Cold cells use
7 fresh processes per seed (21 samples). Jobs use randomized matched blocks;
accepted2 changes the ordering seed. No builds/tests/other prover processes
were run concurrently with timed campaigns. Input generation, preparation,
verification and resource sampling are outside total-prover time.
