Sixteen-worker regression review
2026-10-13; baseline 23c182868e94ff044d5e67a03baed056c4335c16

Observed results supplied by the coordinated campaign
- Falcon-1024, batch 1024, security 128, sixteen workers, repeated untraced
  proving: 48.701 -> 49.309 ms (+1.25%). The independent repeat measured
  48.506 -> 49.284 ms (+1.60%). All seed medians were slower in both comparisons,
  although measurements were noisy. This remains an observed limitation.
- The traced follow-up did not reproduce the slowdown. Forest elapsed time
  improved 21.393 -> 21.006 ms and traced proving 45.854 -> 45.064 ms; binding
  was 6.918 -> 6.908 ms and PCS 3.795 -> 3.834 ms.
- Traced results are diagnostic only. They neither invalidate the untraced
  measurements nor establish the cause of their roughly 0.6-0.8 ms difference.
- Evidence: regression-comparison.txt, regression2-comparison.txt,
  phases/regression-profile.json, runs/regression-profile/, and
  regression-forest-profile.json in this directory.

Source inspection
- Compared the changed forest, kernels, x86 kernels and row dispatcher with
  HEAD 23c182868. No clear actionable cause for the slowdown was identified.
- At sixteen workers, factored_tables_default() is false. The forest builds
  the original expanded tables through fold_table_in, retaining their lengths
  and prescaling thresholds. The x86 fold specialization uses FACTORED=false
  with the original direct table lookups and arithmetic.
- RowDispatch selects the existing CyclicRows implementation. Its row ownership,
  map_jobs ownership transfer, allocations and synchronization are unchanged.
- New source-level dispatch work consists of a selector per Forest, per-level
  representation branches, and a twelve-worker eligibility check before cyclic
  dispatch. RowIndices additionally wraps StepBy for the upper-level construction
  and dense round-sum map_indices consumers. Upper construction writes disjoint
  output subtrees; it is not a read-only stage.
- These observations do not quantify generated-code overhead. No assembly
  inspection or isolated experiment established that the extra dispatch affects
  measured latency. No added large allocation, table expansion or factored lookup
  is selected by the sixteen-worker default path in the source.

Conclusion and limits
- Preserve the repeated untraced slowdown in the reported results. The traced
  follow-up and source inspection provide no attributable cause and do not
  justify a speculative implementation change.
- This was a read-only source review using existing campaign evidence. No source
  edits, builds, tests, proofs or benchmarks were performed for the review.
