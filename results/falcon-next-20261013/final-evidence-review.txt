Diagnostic parser: one actionable strictness gap in the reviewed version.
Location: forest-profile.py:109-118; reused analyze-forest.py:41-60 and 85-99.
The whitelist checks labels that are present, while a block is considered complete
as soon as 'level 0' occurs. Missing internal timing lines are not rejected and
derived categories silently sum/get missing values as zero. For example, a block
with a removed 'L2 jit fold' line still has its valid closing level0/forest/prover
events and would underreport JIT folds. This follows directly from the parser code;
no synthetic execution was run. Raw hash checks protect saved files but do not
prove that the original logger emitted every expected category.
Recommendation: require the expected label set/presence for supported public forest
geometries before deriving totals, with a synthetic dropped-internal-line test.
At minimum, narrow the claim that incomplete blocks fail. This affects diagnostic
stage attribution only, not untraced timings or proof correctness. Root notified.
