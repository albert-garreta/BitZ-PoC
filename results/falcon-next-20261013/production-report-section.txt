Final production qualification: accepted1 and accepted2
Completed-phase supplement; 2026-10-13

Use accepted2 as the main before/after result. Accepted1 is a separate repeat
with the same binaries and geometry, retained below as confirmation. Do not
pool the phases or compare a baseline from one with production from the other.
Final validation is complete:3414 verified proofs across all campaign phases.
Regression/profile/test results and limitations are consolidated in report.txt.

At twelve workers, the final production executable reduces accepted2 total
prover time by 7.98-8.78% across the two degrees and cold/repeated modes. All
three seed medians improve in every twelve-worker cell in both phases.
Accepted1 agrees: its twelve-worker reductions are 8.27-9.89%. At eight workers
the repeated prover changes are within 0.3% in both phases, with mixed seeds;
these measurements establish no material eight-worker prover improvement.

Verification is a visible tradeoff: accepted2 twelve-worker pooled medians
increase by 0.085-0.178 ms (0.92-1.17%). The combined commit+prove+verify median
still improves by 5.97-6.75%. Falcon-512 cold verification increases in every
seed in both phases; repeated verification has mixed seed outcomes. Falcon-1024
cold verification changes sign between phases, while repeated pooled medians
increase in both. Do not describe this result as zero performance regressions.

Practical reading and limits
- accepted2 twelve-worker peak-median deltas range from approximately zero
  to +0.625MiB; retained medians rise +0.176 to +0.707MiB. This does not
  support claiming a large process-memory reduction from compact tables.
- Falcon-512 repeated at twelve workers is29.371ms versus27.905ms at eight;
  twelve remains about5.25% slower for this case. Falcon-1024 repeated is
 50.606ms at twelve versus51.138ms at eight, about1.04% faster. Worker-scaling
  comparisons change public pool/placement and enabled strategy together;
  only the matched before/after cells isolate this production build change.
- All main claims here concern the measured full batch/security/native host.
  Workers1/16, other batches/security settings, final profiles, and final
  correctness/testing totals are reported separately in report.txt.
- This supplement read saved summaries/manifests/results only. It did not
  rerun provers, builds, tests, global validation, or incomplete phase analysis.
- Final validation covers3414 proofs. Proof Debug equality is whole-proof
  value evidence, not serialized-byte comparison.
