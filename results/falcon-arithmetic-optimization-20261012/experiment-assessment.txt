Arithmetic Falcon optimization experiment assessment
Date: 2026-10-12

Decision
--------
Promote ownership of the existing ring/binding buffers only. Keep paired public
evaluation, recurrence group changes, carry fusion, and the additional binding
switches experimental. Keep the factored ring protocol explicitly experimental:
its measured gains are substantial, but it changes the proof relation and
requires its separate version and security analysis.

This assessment uses the completed screen, combinations, qualify, confirm,
profile, and profile_details phases in summary.json and profile-summary.json.
It excludes correctness100 and correctness128 timings: those runs intentionally
overlapped builds and supply correctness evidence only. The final production
benchmark build was still running when this assessment was written; production
binary measurements are not inferred from experimental-build measurements.
No builds or benchmarks were run as part of this independent assessment.

Measurement interpretation
--------------------------
All timing tables below concern batch 1,024 and 128-bit security. Latency cells
have 21 samples across seeds 42, 43, and 44; profile cells have nine samples.
Total prover time includes witness construction and commitment plus proof
generation. Profile stage wall times are inclusive and overlap with parents.

The coordinate diagnostic's public_eval_worker_ns, recurrence_worker_ns, and
carry_worker_ns fields are SUMMED WORKER TIME, converted to milliseconds in
profile-summary.json. They are not elapsed phase times, cannot be added to wall
times, and should not be divided by worker count to claim exact wall savings.
The basis/power and buffer-allocation fields are elapsed wall measurements.

Pooled medians can reverse a comparison across seeds. For example, combinations
owned_public versus owned at N512 has a pooled total-time change of -0.25%,
while all three matched-seed total medians are slower (+0.13%, +0.14%, +0.21%).
Use the per-seed evidence alongside pooled summaries. Small effects are also
subject to run variation: screen control versus original baseline differed by
approximately -3.3% for one N1024 seed. The percentages here are observed median
changes, not confidence intervals or claims of statistical significance.

Ownership is the repeatable production improvement
--------------------------------------------------
Confirm phase, total prover milliseconds:

  Degree  Workers  Original baseline  Owned buffers  Change
     512        1            140.906        139.189   -1.22%
     512        8             33.746         31.894   -5.49%
     512       16             34.476         31.864   -7.58%
    1024        1            276.047        270.129   -2.14%
    1024        8             63.790         56.193  -11.91%
    1024       16             62.199         54.318  -12.67%

Each confirm seed improves in every cell. The qualify phase independently shows
the same direction across the degree/worker matrix. The profiles also show the
expected reduction in binding construction and the first word fold:

  Degree  Workers  Stage                    Control ms  Owned ms
     512       16  Binding construction          1.415     0.440
     512       16  First word fold               2.602     1.297
    1024       16  Binding construction          2.751     0.857
    1024       16  First word fold               5.413     2.336

These child-stage medians are explanatory evidence; do not add them to the
inclusive binding stage or treat their sum as an independently measured total.

The coordinate bottleneck is largely buffer allocation/initialization
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The new profile separates a cost previously included under "coordinates":
allocation and initialization of the large s1/s2 coordinate vectors. On the
canonical control path this is the dominant elapsed component at 8/16 workers:

  Degree  Workers  Coordinates ms  Buffer allocation/initialization ms  Share*
     512        1           4.122                                2.036   49.4%
     512        8           2.706                                2.266   83.8%
     512       16           2.913                                2.523   86.6%
    1024        1           9.066                                4.926   54.3%
    1024        8           6.166                                5.340   86.6%
    1024       16           6.046                                5.323   88.1%

  * Ratio of separately computed medians, not the median of per-trial ratios.

The owned path shows nearly the same coordinate behavior: at 16 workers,
allocation/initialization takes 2.492 of 2.864 ms for N512 and 5.253 of 5.960 ms
for N1024. Basis and shared power setup is only about 0.033/0.063 ms.

This materially changes the interpretation of the old ~3/~6 ms "coordinate
generation" figures. They were not pure recurrence timings. The timed buffer
interval includes allocator and initialization work and may include page faults;
the data does not separate those mechanisms or establish that every byte was
physically zeroed by the same implementation. Avoid calling it a measured
memset-only cost. No allocator/zeroing optimization was qualified in this task.

Paired public evaluation improves worker work but not total proving
------------------------------------------------------------------
At 16 workers, compare profile owned with profile_details owned_public:

  Degree  Public evaluation summed-worker ms  Change  Coordinate wall ms
     512                         1.171 -> 0.394  -66.4%       2.864 -> 2.745
    1024                         2.548 -> 0.913  -64.2%       5.960 -> 6.003

Thus the SIMD dot product reduces measured evaluation work, while the much
larger serial buffer interval limits its effect on the enclosing stage. These
are separate diagnostic phases, so the exact small wall changes should not be
treated as a matched latency result.

The untraced results do not qualify this switch:
  - Screen N512 total medians regress for every seed: +0.45%, +2.59%, +5.54%
    versus the same-build control. N1024 effects are near zero and mixed.
  - Combinations owned_public versus owned remains near zero; N512 is slower
    for all three matched seeds despite its misleading pooled median.

A repeatable memory anomaly occurs in untraced combinations N512: owned_public
peaks at 112,440 KiB versus owned 90,084 KiB, or 109.8 versus 88.0 MiB (+24.8%).
Each seed increases 24.7-25.8%. The prepared power table itself is only 11 KiB at
this degree, so the extra process RSS cannot be explained as the table's direct
size. This assessment does not establish the allocation cause. The anomaly is
not universal: the profile phases report 87,108 KiB for owned_public versus
88,720 KiB for owned. Instrumentation/run-phase differences matter; do not claim
a proven permanent leak or a fixed +25% memory cost on every workload.

Carry fusion is useful mainly at 16 workers, not a universal improvement
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Screen and combinations favored carry fusion, especially N1024 at 16 workers.
The broader qualify and confirm phases explain why it remains experimental.
Changes below compare owned_carries with owned; negative means faster:

  Degree Workers  Qualify total change  Confirm total change
     512       1                +0.25%                +0.11%
     512       8                +0.04%                -1.60%
     512      16                -0.41%                -1.15%
    1024       1                -0.08%                +0.35%
    1024       8                -0.20%                -0.27%
    1024      16                -1.33%                -1.33%

All confirm single-worker seeds regress: N512 +0.096% to +0.139%, N1024 +0.307%
to +0.485%. The N512 8-worker pooled gain is not repeated in qualify. N1024 at
16 workers is the strongest result: qualify improves 0.88-1.75% per seed and
confirm improves 1.19-1.47% per seed. N512's smaller 16-worker gain is less
stable between phases. These observations support a future narrowly qualified
policy experiment, not enabling fusion for every production configuration now.

The diagnostic carry span disappears when fusion is enabled because its work
moves into coordinates. Compare complete ring or total prover time. At 16
workers, profile-details owned_carries reports coordinate wall times of
2.961/6.162 ms and complete ring times of 4.673/9.218 ms for N512/N1024, versus
profile owned 2.864/5.960 ms and ring 4.830/9.530 ms. The moved work is visible;
an absent carry span is not evidence that carry computation became free.

Recurrence group changes are not qualified
-----------------------------------------
Group2 has mixed N512 screen results and only 0.08-0.61% N1024 total gains per
seed. Group8 has mixed results, with no repeatable material benefit across both
degrees. Keep the established group4 default.

The independent assembly review supplies a plausible cost mechanism, not a
performance conclusion: group2 uses 17 ZMM registers with no hot-loop stack
references; group4 uses 24 with two scalar pointer reloads and no vector spills;
group8 uses all 32 and reloads two 64-byte correction vectors each recurrence
iteration, plus fourteen scalar pointers. See assembly-review.txt for the
inspected binary hash, exact commands and instruction addresses. The factored
ring's four-pair adjoint-only kernel had no vector spills in its hot loop.

Additional binding switches and the factored protocol
----------------------------------------------------
Combinations do not justify turning on every candidate together. For example,
owned_all N512 total medians regress for all seeds (+0.50% to +1.25%) relative
to ownership alone; N1024 is mixed and near zero. Factored norm correction and
the word-fold switches remain experiments rather than assumed improvements.

The experimental factored ring is a distinct, promising result. Confirm phase:

  Degree Workers  Owned total ms  Factored total ms  Factored versus owned
     512       1         139.189            128.671                 -7.56%
     512       8          31.894             29.012                 -9.04%
     512      16          31.864             30.048                 -5.70%
    1024       1         270.129            241.744                -10.51%
    1024       8          56.193             52.129                 -7.23%
    1024      16          54.318             51.239                 -5.67%

Its gain persists after comparing against the ownership improvement, so the
entire effect should not be attributed to eliminating clones. Qualify also
shows improvements in all six cells. Confirm verification times are about
13.73/6.65/7.68 ms for N512 and 23.64/9.48/9.82 ms for N1024 at 1/8/16 workers.
The protocol's smaller coordinate representation does not guarantee a lower
whole-process peak than owned binding: at N1024/16, combinations factored RSS
is 3.2-3.6% higher per seed than owned, although below control. At one worker
the factored path's memory saving is much larger.

Matched proof-size differences vary with the new transcript's PCS queries;
they are not exactly the fixed lift-payload increment. In the completed
screen/combinations seeds, increases remain below 0.3%, comfortably below the
10% cap. Security equivalence requires the separate degree-20 projection
accounting and protocol version; performance does not establish soundness.
See security-review.txt. The factored_fast combination offers only a small
N512 improvement and mixed N1024 behavior, so it is not separately qualified.

Remaining interpretation limits
-------------------------------
No result here establishes an instruction-level cause of total speed changes.
The profiles identify buffer allocation/initialization as the next concrete
canonical ring target, while demonstrating why reducing arithmetic worker time
alone was insufficient. Review final production measurements separately once
available, and preserve the experimental qualification boundaries above.
