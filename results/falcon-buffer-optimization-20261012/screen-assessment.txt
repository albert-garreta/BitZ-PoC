Independent assessment of the completed 16-worker screen, 2026-10-12

Evidence: analysis-screen.json/.txt, produced by analyze.py only after all 864
processes completed. Revalidated 1728 proofs including warmups, raw artifact and
command hashes, frozen binary hashes, affinity/metadata, input/source equality,
and exact proof identity within v9/v10 against the frozen baseline. No missing
baseline cases. No prover execution was performed by the analysis helper.

Recommendation: qualify coordinate-direct + word-direct together against
candidate control at 1/8/16 workers, both degrees and protocols, separately for
21 cold and 21 repeated samples. Their combination was not measured in this
screen: the variant called "combined" instead uses coordinate reuse and word
scratch/direct initialization, plus factored kernels for v10.

Individual total-prover median reductions versus candidate control:
  Degree/protocol      Coordinate-direct cold/repeated    Word-direct cold/repeated
  512 / v9                     6.41% / 5.98%                    4.59% / 1.26%
  512 / v10                    3.34% / 1.95%                    4.77% / 3.79%
  1024 / v9                    7.51% / 8.28%                    4.77% / 2.51%
  1024 / v10                   4.02% / 1.76%                    5.66% / 6.58%
Each individual direct variant improves all 24 corresponding seed medians.
Both retain zero cached scratch. Post-drop RSS median changes versus control
range from -588 to +308 KiB for coordinate-direct and -440 to +232 KiB for
word-direct; sample peak-RSS median changes are also below 1 MiB in magnitude.
Minor-fault counts stay approximately unchanged. This supports removal of
initialization traffic as a hypothesis; fault counts alone do not establish
the cause of the latency gains.

Frozen baseline versus candidate control is close: control total medians range
from 0.98% lower to 0.60% higher across the eight matched cells. Candidate control
is the cleaner implementation comparison because it uses the instrumented
candidate binary. Both direct variants also improve every matching frozen
baseline total median. These are screening observations, not confidence bounds.

Other variants:
  Coordinate-zeroed is competitive with coordinate-direct in v9 (5.63-8.75%
  reductions), but v10 repeated gains are only 0.24% and 0.40%, with mixed seed
  directions. Direct is the more consistent choice across both protocols.
  Coordinate reuse retains 22/44 MiB in v9 and about 11/22 MiB in v10. Cold
  proving is slightly worse in all pooled cells. At degree 1024 repeated,
  total medians regress 1.23% in v9 and 1.57% in v10, all three seeds each.
  Degree 512 repeated does improve, so there is workload dependence; do not
  promote reuse globally from this screen.
  Word reuse has a real candidate benefit for repeated v10: 5.44% at degree
  512 and 9.20% at degree 1024, all seeds faster. The tradeoff is 16/32 MiB of
  cached witness scratch, with corresponding retained/peak RSS growth. At
  degree 1024 repeated v9 it is 0.34% slower overall with mixed seed directions.
  Keep it experimental or consider a separate explicit repeated-v10 policy.
  Factored initial fusion and factored fold reduction each change pooled total
  latency by at most 0.51% in magnitude. Their seed directions are not broadly
  consistent; no basis here to promote either arithmetic kernel.
  Reuse-heavy combined gains 5.60-9.03% across the eight cells, but retains
  27-76 MiB of scratch. For degree 1024 repeated it loses to coordinate-direct
  alone in v9 and word-reuse alone in v10. Its v10 verification rises from
  9.799 to 13.743 ms at degree 1024 repeated, while process peak RSS rises from
  about 182 to 228 MiB. It is not a clean default candidate.

Interpretation limits:
  Cold is first commit+prove after setup in a fresh process/Prepared; caches
  are not flushed. Repeated samples share Prepared and process, giving only
  three process clusters despite 21 timed samples. Per-seed agreement is not
  a statistical confidence interval. Post-drop RSS includes inputs, allocator
  retention and Prepared, while cached scratch bytes isolates stored vectors.
  This screen is batch 1024, security 128, 16 workers on the recorded host.
  Additivity and 1/8-worker behavior require the proposed qualification run.
