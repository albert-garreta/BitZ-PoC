Measurement design and limits:
  Cold means first proof in a fresh process and Prepared object; it does not
  mean flushed CPU or filesystem caches. Default: seven processes per each of
  three seeds. Repeated mode shares Prepared across two warmups and seven timed
  samples per seed. Warmups are excluded from reported sample medians.
  getrusage(RUSAGE_SELF) records process-wide minor-fault deltas around commit
  plus prove. Resource sampling, verification, input generation and preparation
  are outside those timers. Fault counts are not converted into guessed bytes.
  Retained RSS is sampled after verification and proof drop, with Prepared,
  input corpus, JSON event and allocator state still live. Peak RSS remains the
  process-lifetime high-water mark. Cached scratch bytes count only additional
  coordinate and word-binding cached vector capacity, excluding pre-existing
  sumcheck scratch, in-flight and allocator-retained memory.
  Phase manifests bind settings, job/repetition lists, runner/helper hashes and
  frozen binary hashes. Resume validates commands and reconstructs raw trials.
  Final validation compares candidate proofs with frozen v9/v10 baselines and
  verifies input/source equality; the two protocols are compared separately.
  Latency harness runs are sequential, pinned and untraced. Separate profiles
  can use the previous arithmetic runner.
  This review did not compile Rust, execute tests, run benchmarks, or establish
  performance improvements. Root owns build/test and benchmark qualification.
