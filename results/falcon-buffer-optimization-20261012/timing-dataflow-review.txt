Conclusion
----------
No newly introduced class of witness-dependent branch, division/remainder, or
memory-address selection was found in the inspected changes. The new factored
first-round fusion DOES call the existing witness-dependent signed_acc helper.
Its zero/sign predicates on a, b, and b-a were already used by the corresponding
separate baseline passes. Their order and grouping change, so this finding is
not a claim that the new function is constant-time or has an identical timing
trace. No overall constant-time certification is made.
