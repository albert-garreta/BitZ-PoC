Method and comparison boundaries
Batch1024/security128, degrees512/1024, 12 workers, seeds42/43/44. Each variant
has 21 cold and 21 repeated measured proofs per degree. Cold uses seven fresh
processes per seed; repeated uses two warmups then seven timed proofs per seed.
Variants run in randomized matched blocks; repeated samples share Prepared.

Conclusion and limits
The completed evidence strongly supports removing the12-worker legacy binder
choice for this host and measured geometry. Same-binary results separate that
effect from build differences and explicitly hold PCS scheduling fixed. The
optional SMT placement provides a further consistent proving gain with the
verification caveat above; it must remain separately labelled.

This phase does not qualify the newly built production executable/defaults,
prove absence of regressions at1/8/16 workers or other batch/security settings,
or establish statistical significance. Final production confirmation remains
required. N512 repeated time is still above22ms in both tested placements.
